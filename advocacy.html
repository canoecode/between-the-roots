Advocacy

The Advocacy Team

The Advocacy Team of BTR strives to advocate for society’s social and environmental problems, through writing articles, creating YouTube videos, and spreading awareness.

The sole focus of the advocacy root revolves around sharing your public voice and support as means to better the community. The power of voice in today’s world is indescribable. Essentially, BTR hopes to hone said voice and emphasize the sheer importance of advocacy in relations to our goals.

Here in the Advocacy Team, we focus on spreading knowledge and sharing our support for environmental and societal issues. Primarily, but not limited, through ways of articles and YouTube videos. The value of advocacy is utilized to its fullest in order to achieve our aims of sustainability.
